# Apple Developer team: what Cup's seat can do, and what moving Sign in with Apple to another team costs

Research date: 2026-11-18, for [issue #471](https://github.com/cup-audio/cup/issues/471) of the [Sign in with Apple map](https://github.com/cup-audio/cup/issues/465). Repository observations are from `main` at `7cf2a405`: both iOS targets, `App` (`audio.cup.app`) and `CupShare` (`audio.cup.app.share`), sign with team `4GQ2V3WJW8`, the individual membership the ticket describes ([`apps/mobile-app/AGENTS.md`](../../../apps/mobile-app/AGENTS.md), [`apps/mobile-app/README.md`](../../../apps/mobile-app/README.md)). Every source below is an Apple page read on the research date. Apple's Account Help and App Store Connect Help pages carry no revision date (only a 2026 copyright footer); TN3159's revision history ends at 2024-02-07. Nothing was tried in the Apple Developer account: which role Cup's seat holds, and whether a build of `audio.cup.app` was ever uploaded to App Store Connect, are not known from here and are marked _to check_.

## Question

Cup's only Apple access is a seat on someone else's individual Apple Developer Program membership. What can that seat do (certificates, identifiers, keys, Services IDs, App Store Connect, agreements), what does the App Store show as seller, and what does enrolling an individual or organization membership take in 2026? What does transferring an app with Sign in with Apple to another team cost: team-scoped user identifiers, transfer identifiers and migration, and what users lose if migration is skipped?

## Answer

**A seat on an individual membership is an App Store Connect user, not a member of the developer team, so it cannot touch Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles.** Apple: "If you're enrolled as an individual and add users in App Store Connect, users receive access only to your content in App Store Connect and are not considered part of your team in the Apple Developer Program" ([Roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/roles/)), and "Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles is only available to Account Holders and members of an organization's team" (same page). So every Sign in with Apple setup step in the Apple Developer console (enabling the capability on the App ID, registering the Services ID for the web, creating the Sign in with Apple key, configuring the Private Email Relay, creating distribution certificates and provisioning profiles) is the Account Holder's to do on an individual membership. The seat can at most do App Store Connect work its role allows. Agreements and app transfers are the Account Holder's alone on any membership.

**The seller shown on the App Store for an individual membership is the Account Holder's personal legal name**; for an organization it is the legal entity's name ([Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/)).

**Enrolling costs 99 USD a year either way.** An individual needs an Apple Account with two-factor authentication, legal name and address, and possibly a photo ID, and buys the membership at enrollment. An organization must be a legal entity (no DBAs or trade names) with a D-U-N-S Number, a website and a work email on its domain, and an enrolling person with binding authority; it pays only after Apple Developer Support has verified it. D-U-N-S: up to 5 business days from Dun & Bradstreet plus up to 2 business days for Apple to receive it. Apple publishes no total verification time. An individual membership can be converted to an organization membership on request, if the Account Holder is the organization's founder or cofounder ([Updating your account information](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/updating-your-account-information/)).

**Moving an app with Sign in with Apple to another team changes every user's identifier and every relay address**, because both are scoped to the developer team. Apple provides a migration: before or within 60 days after the transfer, the old team turns each stored user identifier into a transfer identifier, hands those over, and the new team exchanges them for its own identifiers and relay addresses. It needs the stored identifier of every user and a client secret signed with each team's Sign in with Apple key. A user who is not migrated is, to Apple and to Supabase, a new person to the new team: the old account can't be matched by Apple identifier, and a Hide My Email address changes too. After 60 days the endpoints close; the documented remedy is transferring the app back and forth again.

**An app can only be transferred once it has a version released on the App Store** ([App transfer criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/app-transfer-criteria/)), and an explicit App ID "for an app you uploaded to App Store Connect" can't be deleted ([Delete an App ID](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/delete-an-app-id/)). So once a build of `audio.cup.app` is uploaded under team `4GQ2V3WJW8`, that bundle identifier can leave the team only through a full app transfer after a public release. Deciding the team before the first upload, not only before the first real Apple sign-in, keeps the bundle identifier movable.

## What a seat on an individual membership can do

### Individual versus organization teams

- Individual: "you can give up to 50 additional users access to your content in App Store Connect. These users only access App Store Connect—they're not part of your team and won't receive other membership benefits." Organization: "you can add members to your team. All users receive access to App Store Connect and all other membership resources and benefits" ([Add and edit users](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/add-and-edit-users/); the same in [Overview of accounts and roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/overview-of-accounts-and-roles/)).
- The Roles page opens with "If you're enrolled as an organization, you have the option of adding additional members to your team" and describes the role tables for "each organization" ([Roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/roles/)). [Invite team members](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/invite-team-members/) likewise begins "Organizations have the option of adding additional people to their development team."
- Access to Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles is "Granted as a separate permission in Users and Access", with the footnote "Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles is only available to Account Holders and members of an organization's team" ([Roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/roles/)). On an individual membership that permission therefore cannot be given to anyone but the Account Holder.

### Developer console tasks (Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles)

From the organization role table on [Roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/roles/), read from the page's HTML because the checkmarks are icons. Admin, App Manager and Developer need the separate Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles permission for these rows; on an individual membership only the Account Holder column applies.

| Task                                        | Account Holder | Admin | App Manager | Developer |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ----- | ----------- | --------- |
| Accept legal agreements, renew membership   | Yes            | No    | No          | No        |
| Create and revoke development certificates  | Yes            | Yes   | Yes         | Yes       |
| Create and revoke distribution certificates | Yes            | Yes   | Yes         | No        |
| Create and delete distribution profiles     | Yes            | Yes   | Yes         | No        |
| Download provisioning profiles              | Yes            | Yes   | Yes         | Yes       |
| Register and configure App IDs              | Yes            | Yes   | Yes         | No        |
| Create keys                                 | Yes            | Yes   | Yes         | No        |

Sign in with Apple's own help pages are stricter than the table: [About Sign in with Apple](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/capabilities/about-sign-in-with-apple/) and [Delete an App ID](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/delete-an-app-id/) say "Required role: Account Holder or Admin." The Sign in with Apple key is created and associated with a primary App ID, at most two keys per primary App ID ([Create a Sign in with Apple private key](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/capabilities/create-a-sign-in-with-apple-private-key/)). Services IDs for the web are registered in the same console ([Register a Services ID](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/identifiers/register-a-services-id/) is listed under Identifiers).

Consequence for Cup: on team `4GQ2V3WJW8`, the Account Holder personally does the Sign in with Apple capability, the Services ID with its domain and return URL, the key (and hands its `.p8` to Cup for Supabase's client secret), the Private Email Relay registration, and the distribution certificate and profiles for release signing. Xcode automatic signing for a device or the App Store "needs an Xcode account in that team" ([`apps/mobile-app/README.md`](../../../apps/mobile-app/README.md)), which an App Store Connect user of an individual membership is not, by Apple's wording above. _To check:_ how the seat currently signs device builds, and which App Store Connect role it holds.

### App Store Connect tasks

From the App Store Connect role tables on [Roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/access/roles/): Admin, App Manager and Developer can upload builds; Admin and App Manager can create app versions, submit apps, manage TestFlight builds and external groups, and manage users; Developer can manage internal TestFlight groups; creating app records needs a separate permission for Developer. Only the Account Holder signs agreements ("Sign agreements"), transfers apps ("Transfer apps"), and removes auto-renewable subscriptions from sale. The Account Holder also accepts updated license agreements; until then the team loses "full access to membership resources, such as Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles, App Store Connect, and the App Store Connect API" (same page).

### Account Holder role

"Account Holder transfers for individual members are granted on a limited basis and require assistance from Apple" ([Updating your account information](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/updating-your-account-information/)). There is no documented self-service way to hand an individual membership to someone else.

## Seller name

- Individual: "your personal legal name will be listed as the seller on the App Store" ([Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/)). Changing the name on the Apple Account "will not change the name used for your membership or seller name" ([Updating your account information](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/updating-your-account-information/)).
- Organization: "the legal entity name will be listed as the seller on the App Store", for example "Seller: ABC Company, Inc." ([Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/)). A sole proprietor "must join as an individual and your legal name will appear as the seller" ([Enrollment support](https://developer.apple.com/support/enrollment/)).

## Enrolling in 2026

| Item              | Individual                                                                                                     | Organization                                                                                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Fee               | 99 USD a year, local currency where available                                                                  | Same                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Account           | Apple Account with two-factor authentication, legal age                                                        | Same; Apple recommends the organization's email address                                                                                                                  |
| Identity          | Legal name, phone, address (no P.O. box); may be asked for a government ID number or photo ID; own credit card | Legal entity (no DBAs, fictitious businesses, trade names or branches), D-U-N-S Number, binding authority (or a reference who confirms it), possibly notarized documents |
| Website and email | Not required                                                                                                   | Public, functional website on the organization's domain; work email on that domain                                                                                       |

Sources: [Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/), [Identity verification](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/identity-verification/), [Enrollment requirements](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/), [Overview of accounts and roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/overview-of-accounts-and-roles/).

**D-U-N-S.** Free "in most jurisdictions". Look the organization up first; if it is missing, request one from Dun & Bradstreet. "Please allow up to 5 business days to receive your number from D&B. Expediting your D‑U‑N‑S Number creation process will not shorten this waiting period", then "up to 2 business days for Apple to receive your information from D&B". If Apple reports "Your organization is not listed as a legal entity", the D&B record has a different legal status, such as sole proprietorship ([D-U-N-S Number](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/)).

**Duration.** Apple states no overall verification time. The only figures are the D-U-N-S ones above and "If you haven't received a membership confirmation within 24 hours of your purchase, contact us" ([Program enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/program-enrollment/)). Developer Forums threads from 2026 report individual-to-organization conversions pending for months; they are anecdotes, not Apple statements.

**Individual to organization later.** "If you'd like to update your membership from an individual to an organization, please submit a request. You'll need to be the founder/cofounder of the organization and provide details such as your organization's D-U-N-S Number. You also may be asked to submit business documents" ([Updating your account information](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/updating-your-account-information/)). Apple's pages do not say whether the Team ID survives the conversion; since the conversion changes the membership rather than moving apps, it presumably does, which would keep Sign in with Apple identifiers unchanged (_unverified_, ask Apple Developer Support before relying on it). Renaming an organization later "will update your vendor name across all your apps" and resets `identifierForVendor` (same page).

## Moving an app with Sign in with Apple to another team

### Identifiers are team-scoped

"Every Sign in with Apple user has a user identifier. Apple issues a signed ID token for the user that contains the user identifier and, if applicable, the private email address specific to your team. All of your applications share the same identifier for the user" ([Transferring your apps and users to another team](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/transferring-your-apps-and-users-to-another-team)). The recipient side: "The user identifier and the private email address are specific to your team" ([Bringing new apps and users into your team](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/bringing-new-apps-and-users-into-your-team)).

### App transfer prerequisites

- "The app must have at least one version that was released to the App Store"; it can't be on pre-order or in review; both accounts must not be "in a pending or changing state" and must have accepted the latest agreements ([App transfer criteria](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/app-transfer-criteria/)).
- The Account Holder initiates and the receiving Account Holder accepts. The app keeps its bundle ID, reviews and ratings, and stays downloadable ([Overview of app transfer](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer/)).
- Sign in with Apple specifics on the same page: generate a transfer identifier for each user before the transfer; ungroup grouped apps; the associated Services ID transfers with the app unless its association is removed first. TestFlight must be turned off for all beta versions before the transfer.
- Keychain sharing (Cup's app and Share Extension may share a keychain group; _to check_): "Keychain sharing continues to work only until the app is updated", the group must be replaced with one carrying the recipient's Team ID, and "users must re-login once as the app cannot retrieve the authentication token from the keychain" (same page). App groups are re-registered on the recipient's account after the transfer.

### Migration with transfer identifiers

Both sides use `https://appleid.apple.com` ([sending side](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/transferring-your-apps-and-users-to-another-team), [receiving side](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/bringing-new-apps-and-users-into-your-team), [TN3159](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3159-migrating-sign-in-with-apple-users-for-an-app-transfer)):

1. Old team gets an access token: `POST /auth/token` with `grant_type=client_credentials`, `scope=user.migration`, the app's `client_id` (App ID or Services ID) and a `client_secret` JWT signed with the old team's key. TN3159: no rate limiting, so users can be migrated in batches.
2. Old team calls `POST /auth/usermigrationinfo` with `sub` (the old team-scoped identifier) and `target` (the recipient's Team ID) for each stored user, and gets a `transfer_sub`. The transfer identifier is one-to-one with the old identifier, specific to the recipient team, and stable across later transfers to the same team.
3. Old team exports its user data keyed by `transfer_sub` only, excluding old identifiers and relay addresses, then initiates the app transfer.
4. New team gets its own access token (same call, its own client secret) and calls `POST /auth/usermigrationinfo` with `transfer_sub`, receiving the new `sub`, the new team's relay `email` if the user hid theirs, and `is_private_email`.
5. Users who sign in after the transfer bring a `transfer_sub` claim in their ID token "for up to 60 days from the transfer date"; on device, `getCredentialState` returns `.transferred`, and a silent re-authorization delivers the new identifier.

Timing: the old team "can generate transfer identifiers before the recipient team accepts the app transfer, or up to 60 days after", and the recipient "needs to exchange the transfer identifiers ... within 60 days of accepting the app transfer". After 60 days "the ID token no longer includes the transfer identifier. Also, the server endpoint used to obtain the team-scoped identifiers becomes inactive." TN3159's remedy for a missed window: transfer the app back to the original team and forward again, which reopens the endpoints.

What the migration needs from Cup, read against the steps above: every Apple user's old `sub`, which Supabase keeps as the Apple identity's provider ID; working Sign in with Apple keys on both teams; and the old team's cooperation for the calls in steps 1 to 3, which on an individual membership means the Account Holder or a key the Account Holder created.

### What users lose without migration

- A different `sub`: the old Cup account can't be found by Apple identifier. A hidden relay address also changes with the team, so even email-based matching fails for users who hid their email; they arrive as strangers and get a new, empty account (_inference_ for Cup: Supabase's identity-linking rules were not re-read for this research).
- The old relay address: "If a user doesn't use any other applications from your team, you'll no longer be able to communicate with them using their private email address after the transfer" ([sending side](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/signinwithapple/transferring-your-apps-and-users-to-another-team)). Account deletion notices and recovery mail to those users stop arriving.
- The users' Apple refresh tokens were issued to the old team's client; whether the new team can still revoke them for account deletion is not stated by Apple (_unverified_).

### Before any App Store release

The app transfer route does not exist until a version is released. An uploaded app's explicit App ID can't be deleted, so a team change before release means a new bundle identifier, or waiting for release and transferring. If no build has been uploaded yet (_to check_), the Account Holder can delete the App IDs, and the new team can register `audio.cup.app` and `audio.cup.app.share` itself. Sign in with Apple users created during that time (TestFlight testers) have no migration path either way, since migration rides on an app transfer.
