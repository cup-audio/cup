# Google Play developer account type for an individual developer in Austria

Research date: 2026-11-16. Question: an individual developer living in Austria wants to publish Cup (website <https://cup.audio>) on Google Play; should the Play Console developer account be personal or organisation, and what would an organisation account cost in Austria? Web sources were read on the research date; nothing was signed up for or requested. Points marked _unverified_ are inferences or come from sources that did not state them directly. This is not legal or tax advice.

## Answer

Start with a **personal account**, unless the developer already runs, or wants for other reasons, a business with its own D-U-N-S number. Google requires an organisation account only for financial, health, VPN and government apps, none of which Cup is. An organisation account needs a D-U-N-S number whose name and address exactly match the payments profile, a verified website and a published phone number, and in Austria it means at least a trade registration (Gewerbeanmeldung) with its social insurance and chamber consequences. The privacy advantage of a personal account is smaller than it looks: a personal account that monetises shows its full address, and from 1 March 2027 a personal account declared as a trader also shows a phone number and address. The main cost of a personal account is the closed test with 12 testers for 14 days before production access. A personal account can later be converted to an organisation account in Play Console, so the choice is not final.

## 1. Google Play account types

### Who needs an organisation account

Google's account-type page says to choose a personal account "if your account is for personal use", naming students, hobbyists and amateur developers, and an organisation account for "an organization or business" with commercial, industrial, professional or governmental activities. Both types have the same core functionality and can monetise through a payments profile. Source: [Choose a developer account type](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885?hl=en).

An organisation account is mandatory only for four app categories: financial products and services (banking, loans, trading, crypto wallets and exchanges), health apps (medical apps, human subjects research), apps approved to use `VpnService`, and government apps. An audio-narration app like Cup is in none of them. Source: [Choose a developer account type](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885?hl=en).

Google names no accepted legal entity types and does not mention sole proprietors anywhere in the pages read. Whether an Austrian Einzelunternehmen counts as an organisation is therefore decided in practice by whether it has a D-U-N-S record and documents that pass verification (below), _unverified_ against Google directly. Apple, by contrast, tells sole proprietors to enrol as individuals ("If your legal status is a sole proprietorship/single person business, enroll as an individual"), which is Apple's rule, not Google's. Sources: [Choose a developer account type](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13634885?hl=en), [Verify your developer identity information](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920?hl=en), [Apple: D-U-N-S Number](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/).

### What an organisation account requires

An organisation account needs a developer name, a D-U-N-S number, the organisation's name and address (taken from the linked Google payments profile), an organisation phone number and website, a contact name, email and phone, a developer email and phone, and the trader status where required. "You can't create an organization developer account without a valid D-U-N-S number"; only known government organisations are exempt. "The legal name and address in your Google payments profile must exactly match the details in your Dun & Bradstreet profile." Source: [Required information to create a Play Console developer account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en).

Organisation accounts without an already verified payments profile submit a D-U-N-S number, an official government identity document of a representative and an official organisation document. Organisation accounts also verify their website, which is a prerequisite for phone verification. Source: [Verify your developer identity information](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10841920?hl=en).

For Austria, Google's country page lists these organisation registration documents: an "Extract from commercial register (example: Firmenbuchauszug)", a "VAT registration certificate" or a "Trade license"; government bodies may use an official letter. A trade licence would plausibly be met by a GISA extract for an Einzelunternehmen without Firmenbuch entry, _unverified_. Individuals use a passport, national ID card, driving licence or residence permit, and a proof of address dated within 90 days. Source: [Required documents by country and region, Austria](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/15633622?hl=en&co=GENIE.CountryCode%3DAT).

### What Google Play shows publicly

Personal account: "Google displays your legal name, your country (based on your legal address), and your developer email address on Google Play." Also: "If you monetize on Google Play, Google displays your full address." Personal accounts need a verified phone number only if they have trader status. Source: [Required information to create a Play Console developer account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en).

Organisation account: "Google displays your legal name, D-U-N-S number, legal address, developer email address, and developer phone number on Google Play." The separate organisation phone number is used for verification and not displayed. Source: [Required information to create a Play Console developer account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en).

Trader status (EEA and UK consumer law): every active account must declare whether it is a trader or non-trader by 1 February 2027, and new accounts on creation. A trader is "any natural or legal person acting for purposes relating to their trade, business, craft or profession"; receiving or intending to receive remuneration on a regular or commercial basis is one of four non-exhaustive criteria, so monetising does not automatically make a developer a trader. For a trader with an individual account, "a phone number and address will now be displayed", from 1 March 2027; a trader's D-U-N-S number is shown under "App support". For non-traders, EEA and UK consumers are told that consumer-protection rights do not apply to their contracts. Failing to declare can block updates and remove apps; misrepresenting the status can suspend the account. Source: [Trader status declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17594752?hl=en).

### Testing requirement for personal accounts

"Google Play requires personal developer accounts created after November 13, 2023, to test their apps": a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days before applying for production access. The page does not mention organisation accounts; that organisation accounts are exempt is an inference from the page's scope, _unverified_, and so is whether converting a personal account to an organisation removes the requirement. Source: [App testing requirements for new personal developer accounts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en).

### Converting a personal account and transferring apps

A personal account can become an organisation account. Prerequisites: the account is "fully verified and show[s] no pending verification warnings", and the owner provides and verifies an official organisation website, after which "the option to update your account type will become available". The owner then creates a new organisation payments profile with the organisation's D-U-N-S number, type, size and phone, verifies its identity if required, links the profile and confirms. Google asks to "wait at least 72 hours after the transition is complete before you submit any new apps". The reverse is impossible: "You can't change the account type from an organization to an individual account." Source: [Update developer identity details managed by a Google payments profile](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16260648?hl=en).

Apps can also be transferred to another developer account. Both accounts must be registered and active, both and the apps must comply with policy, and a target that has paid apps or in-app products needs an active payments profile; a new target account costs the US$25 registration fee. Google transfers "all users, statistics, data, comments, ratings, subscriptions", ratings, reviews, content ratings and store listings, but not test groups (closed tests must be recreated) or bulk exports, payout and earnings reports. The page does not restrict transfers between personal and organisation accounts. Source: [Transfer apps to a different developer account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247?hl=en).

## 2. Austrian routes to a qualifying entity

### Einzelunternehmen with a free trade

Software development falls under the free trade (freies Gewerbe) "Dienstleistungen in der automatischen Datenverarbeitung und Informationstechnik", which needs no proof of qualification. WKO's trade profile includes producing and distributing software products; whether selling one's own consumer app with in-app content is fully covered is not stated, and WKO recommends confirming a specific business model with the Fachverband UBIT or the trade authority, _unverified_ for Cup's model. Sources: [WKO: Unternehmensgründung für Informationstechnologen](https://www.wko.at/information-consulting/unternehmensberatung-buchhaltung-informationstechnologie/it-dienstleistung/unternehmensgruendung), [WKO: Berufsbild Informationstechnologen](https://www.wko.at/oe/information-consulting/unternehmensberatung-buchhaltung-informationstechnologie/it-dienstleistung/berufsbild-informationstechnologen.pdf).

The Gewerbeanmeldung itself is free ("Die Gewerbeanmeldung ist kostenlos") and can be filed online through GISA or the USP e-Gründung with ID Austria; the authority sends a GISA extract. Sources: [WKO: Gründungskosten](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/gruendungskosten), [USP: Gewerbeanmeldung](https://www.usp.gv.at/gruendung/EAP/gewerbeanmeldung.html).

A trade licence brings two running costs. First, compulsory GSVG insurance with the SVS from the day the licence is obtained; a small-business exemption (Kleinunternehmerausnahme) applies on application if 2026 self-employment income stays at or below €6,613.20 and total turnover at or below €55,000, among other conditions, and accident insurance stays. Second, WKO membership with a Grundumlage set per Fachgruppe and state: for the Fachgruppe UBIT in Lower Austria in 2026 a fixed €122 per member, doubled for legal persons; new members are exempt in the calendar year after they first obtain a licence. Kammerumlage 1 is not due while domestic net turnover stays at or below €150,000 a year. Sources: [SVS: Kleinunternehmer](https://www.svs.at/cdscontent/?contentid=10007.846813&portal=svsportal), [USP: Personen mit Gewerbeberechtigung](https://www.usp.gv.at/themen/mitarbeiter-und-gesundheit/einstellung-mitarbeiter-und-arten-der-beschaeftigung/weitere-informationen-einstellen-von-personal/personen-mit-gewerbeberechtigung.html), [WKNÖ: Verlautbarung der Grundumlagen 2026](https://www.wko.at/noe/wko/verlautbarung-wknoe-grundumlage-2026.pdf), [WKO: Kammerumlagen FAQ](https://www.wko.at/steuern/kammerumlagen-faq).

### e.U. (Firmenbuch entry)

Firmenbuch registration of a sole proprietor is voluntary ("Grundsätzlich ist die Anmeldung einer:eines Einzelunternehmer:in zum Firmenbuch freiwillig") and becomes mandatory only after net turnover exceeds €700,000 in two consecutive years, or €1 million in one year. It can be filed fully online with one's own electronic ID and without notarial certification, attaching a specimen-signature declaration (Musterzeichnungserklärung). WKO puts the fee at about €100, waived for new businesses under the NeuFöG with a WKO consultation confirmation. The processing time is not stated, _unverified_. An e.U. may trade under a firm name with the "e.U." suffix, and its Firmenbuchauszug is the document type Google names as an example. Sources: [Justiz: Online-Anmeldung von Einzelunternehmer:innen beim Firmenbuch](https://www.justiz.gv.at/service/datenbanken/firmenbuch/online-anmeldung-von-einzelunternehmer-innen-beim-firmenbuch.bbc.de.html), [WKO: Gründungskosten](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/gruendungskosten), [WKO: Auftritt im Geschäftsverkehr](https://www.wko.at/wirtschaftsrecht/auftreten-geschaeftsverkehr-firma).

### GmbH

Since 1 January 2024 the minimum share capital is €10,000, half of it paid in cash at formation. The GmbH comes into existence on Firmenbuch registration, needs a notarial deed for its articles, must keep accounts under company law and file its annual financial statements with the Firmenbuch within nine months, pays 23% corporate tax and at least €500 a year minimum corporate tax (Mindest-KöSt), and its managing directors' pay is generally subject to social insurance. Source: [WKO: Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung](https://www.wko.at/wirtschaftsrecht/gesellschaft-mit-beschraenkter-haftung-gmbh).

A single natural person who will be sole shareholder and sole managing director can use the simplified formation (§ 9a GmbHG) without a notary: a participating Austrian bank opens the capital account, identifies the founder and sends the bank confirmation and specimen signature to the Firmenbuch, and the founder submits the standardised declaration through the USP with ID Austria. Later amendments still need a notarial deed. Court fees apply unless the NeuFöG waives them. WKO gives about €100–150 for a one-person declaration through a notary and about €450 for the Firmenbuch. Accounting and tax-adviser fees are additional and not quantified by these sources, _unverified_. Sources: [WKO: Vereinfachte Gründung](https://www.wko.at/wirtschaftsrecht/vereinfachte-gruendung-gmbh), [USP: eGründung](https://www.usp.gv.at/services/egruendung.html), [WKO: Gründungskosten](https://www.wko.at/gruendung/gruendungskosten).

## 3. D-U-N-S number in Austria

Dun & Bradstreet's Austrian request page lets Austrian companies apply for a D-U-N-S number or change their record "kostenfrei". It does not state eligibility, required data or processing time. Source: [D&B: D-U-N-S Nummer für ein österreichisches Unternehmen beantragen](https://www.dnb.com/de-at/duns-request/).

D&B's DACH factsheet says the number is assigned to all kinds of businesses, "einschliesslich Einzelunternehmen". So a sole proprietor can in principle get one; whether D&B Austria creates one for an Einzelunternehmen without Firmenbuch entry, based on a GISA extract, is _unverified_. Private persons without a business are not covered. Source: [D&B: Factsheet D-U-N-S Nummer](https://www.dnb.com/content/dam/bisnode/international/pdf_docs/DACH_FS_D-U-N-S-Nr_external_DE.pdf).

Timing: Google says the D-U-N-S process "can take up to 30 days". Apple's enrolment lookup tool searches D&B and, when no record exists, submits a free request; Apple says to allow up to 5 business days for D&B and 2 more for Apple to receive it. D&B's US page mentions a paid expedited option; Google warns against third parties charging for the free number. Many businesses already have a number, so look it up first. Sources: [Android Developers Blog, July 2023](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2023/07/boosting-trust-and-transparency-in-google-play.html), [Apple: D-U-N-S Number](https://developer.apple.com/help/account/membership/D-U-N-S/), [Required information to create a Play Console developer account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en).

## 4. Personal account public information when monetising

Yes, it differs. Without monetising and as a non-trader, a personal account shows legal name, country and developer email. Once it monetises, "Google displays your full address". As a trader with an individual account, from 1 March 2027 a phone number and address are also displayed. Monetising also requires a merchant (payments) profile and a verified payment method. Sources: [Required information to create a Play Console developer account](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13628312?hl=en), [Trader status declaration](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17594752?hl=en).

## Trade-off for this developer

A personal account costs only the US$25 fee and can start now, but needs the 12-tester, 14-day closed test, and once Cup charges money or the developer is a trader, the developer's home address (and as a trader, phone) becomes public, the same as for an organisation. An organisation account removes the testing hurdle (_unverified_) and presents a business identity, but needs a D-U-N-S number matching the payments profile, a verified website (cup.audio would do), a public phone number and an Austrian business: at minimum a free Gewerbeanmeldung, which brings SVS insurance (unless exempt) and WKO fees, and an Einzelunternehmen still uses the owner's legal name and, unless a separate business address is used, the home address. A GmbH separates liability and shows the company's name and address, at €5,000 paid-in capital, €500 a year Mindest-KöSt and accounting costs. Because conversion from personal to organisation exists and keeps the apps, a personal account now and conversion later, once Cup is a business, loses little.
